# Critical product review

## Keep primary

- Today: one word, one thought, one useful action.
- Learn: review queue, search, mastery, and pronunciation.
- Reflect: writing, history, bookmarks, and optional prompts.
- Library: saved and browsable editorial content.
- Profile: evidence of learning and reflection, not public-status theatre.

## Keep secondary or experimental

- future messages;
- weekly recap;
- widgets;
- optional generated reflection guidance.

## Do not promote without proof

- community challenges and leaderboards;
- social circles and collaborative messaging;
- large XP, badge, and cosmetic economies;
- therapeutic positioning;
- opaque personality or emotional-scoring systems;
- multiple overlapping daily rituals.

## UI direction

The supplied references are useful for material, depth, onboarding pacing, and profile composition, but not for product structure. Kairos should borrow:

- translucent navigation and compact control clusters;
- cinematic profile hierarchy;
- large editorial type and disciplined whitespace;
- layered page transitions and restrained parallax;
- tactile pressed states and shared-element continuity.

Kairos should reject:

- feed density designed for social/casino products;
- decorative glass on long reading surfaces;
- neon gradients on every card;
- animation that delays reading;
- productivity dashboards, task counts, and fake urgency;
- AI labels used as a substitute for a clear user benefit.

## Recommendation strategy

Use deterministic, explainable ranking until there is enough interaction data for offline evaluation. Optimize for retained learning and useful engagement, not screen time. Every recommendation should be traceable to review urgency, level fit, stated interests, novelty, diversity, quality, or explicit feedback.

## Generated guidance strategy

Generated guidance should be optional, bounded, and transparent. It must not diagnose, claim human memory, impersonate a spiritual authority, or encourage emotional dependency. The app should remain fully valuable when generation is disabled or offline.
